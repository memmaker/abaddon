Plan for the 7drl "Abaddon"
 - We reverse the direction of the crawl, so the player is going to start in hell
 - Remove: Classes, Mana, Regeneration
 - Core idea: Try dealing more damage than you receive by using AoE and chain combos effectively.
 - Core interactions:
   - Clever use of relocation in combination with aoe
   - Setup & Triggering of chained effects
   - Bonus abilities triggered by movement patterns
   - Change the environment to your advantage
   -> Optimal play would mean moving in the right patterns as to maximize bonus abilities
   -> While at the same time setting up enemies for a chain combo/area of effect ability
   -> NOTE: Enable keyboard only interactions and fast activation of all abilities

 - We want to support these basic play styles:
   - All in melee damage build -> maximize sword of vengeance use
   - Ranged/tactical build -> the main focus is on ranged abilities for relocation/terrain modification
   - Companion build -> the main focus is on the horse abilities
   - Defensive build -> the main focus is on mitigating damage and crowd control
   -> We'll let the player choose from three of the four areas when leveling up

 - Player is his own light source - You literally burn yourself.
   - Decide how many hp you want to spend per step for more light radius

 - Interesting upgrades for the player:
   - Additional effects for the sword of vengeance, that would depend on it's damage
     -> Create a very powerful sword
     - Fire/Ice/Lightning procs
     - Status effect procs
     - Add bonus AC when using it in melee
     - Actor relocation
   - Additional effects that proc during the free horse maneuvers
     - Fire/Ice/Lightning procs
     - Status effect procs
     - Terrain alteration
     -> Create a very powerful companion
   - Direct use abilities
     -> Use the tools to maximize your damage output
     - All of the abilities?
   - Any form of damage mitigation
     -> Defensive strategy

 - Ice Spells: Damage + 50% Slow or 25% Freeze
 - Fire Spells: Damage + 50% Damage over Time
 - Lightning Spells: Damage + 50% Chain Lightning
 - We want abilities that provide the following tools in an interesting and varied manner:
   - Relocate actors
     - Shuffle - Randomly repositions everyone on this floor
     - Paired teleport - Teleports you and one target to random locations
     - Swap Position - Swaps your position with a target
     - Pull - Pulls a target towards you
     - Push - Pushes a target away from you
     - Implosion - Pulls all targets towards a target position
     - Explosion - Pushes all targets away from a target position
     - Gust of Wind - Pushes all targets in the same direction
   - Deal damage to specific areas
     - Circle
     - Line
     - Cone
     - Reflecting ray
   - Crowd control (Sleep, Immobilize, Pacify, Silence, Immolation)
   - Altering the environment
     - Earthquake - Flips walls/floors randomly
     - Floor break - Creates a chasm
     - Ceiling break - Will destroy the ceiling to create random walls and transport monster from above to this level
     - Flatten - Remove all walls in an area
     - Create oil - Will create a slippery, flammable area
     - Create fire - Will create a damaging area
     - Create water - Will create a slowing area
     - Create spikes - Will create a damaging area
     - Pacify - Remove all hazards & neutralize all enemies
     - Raise Hell - Create random hazards and make all enemies begin in-fighting

 - 6 medium to big sized dungeon levels with a unique enemy on each one.
   1. Hell - cerberus - man's best friend - changing from dog icon to a bloodied 'd' when near death, a persistent and fast threat
   2. Dungeons of Doom - a rogue - as '@' - a powerful single enemy with random skills
   3. Caves - looting adventurers - the hobo murderer party - catch: a whole party with skills
   4. Crypt - crusaders - the avatar of lies - uses illusions to waste the resources of the player
   5. Cathedral - false prophets - the inquisitor - uses holy magic/water, will heal allies
   6. Town - evil king and his soldiers - can summon enemies and cast powerful spells

 - Award a unique item after every dungeon level and let the player choose between 3 options
   -> We need at least 6*3 = 18 uniques to make this interesting

 - Environmental interactions
   - Exploding barrels
   - Chasms
   - Spiked Pits/Walls
     - Enemies can be pushed into them
   - Fire/Lava
     - Can be turned into stone with an ice spell, neutralizing its effects, creating random walls
     - Can be extended by sprinkling oil nearby
   - Water
     - Small to medium Enemies in the water can be drowned by moving unto them with the horse
     - Movement through water is slower
     - Can be turned into Unholy Water, dealing damage to "good" enemies on touch
     - Can be turned into boiling Water with a fire spell, dealing damage to living things
     - Can be turned into ice with a spell, enabling further knockback effects
