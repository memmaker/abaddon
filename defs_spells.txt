== Big List of Spells

Fire - Damage over Time

Ice - Freeze

Lighting - Chain

Gravitation - Force flying enemies to the ground, trigger traps!

Immolation - Make enemies explode on death

Shuffle - Randomly repositions everyone on this floor

Paired teleport - Teleports you and one target to random locations

Swap Position - Swaps your position with a target

Pull - Pulls a target towards you

Push - Pushes a target away from you

Implosion - Pulls all targets towards a target position

Explosion - Pushes all targets away from a target position

Gust of Wind - Pushes all targets in the same direction

Floor break - Creates a chasm

Ceiling break - Will destroy the ceiling to create random walls and transport monster from above to this level

Flatten - Remove all walls in an area

Create oil - Will create a slippery, flammable area

Create fire - Will create a damaging area

Create water - Will create a slowing area

Create spikes - Will create a damaging area

Pacify - Remove all hazards & neutralize all enemies

Raise Hell - Create random hazards and make all enemies begin in-fighting

Jumpstart

Multi-Shot

Telefrag

Earthquake

Earth Shaker

Inverter Walls<->Floors

Inverter Water<->Lava

Firestarter: Trigger all visible barrels

Last rite
Self-destruct in gory explosion

Hooves of Hell
Move towards an enemy and then away from him when he is next to you. Trigger horse kick with knockback.

Pounding
Move in a straight line towards an enemy twice, a third move in the same direction can now pin an enemy on the target tile.

Thunderstruck
Pay some health and wait a turn for an aoe lightning attack on all neighboring tiles
